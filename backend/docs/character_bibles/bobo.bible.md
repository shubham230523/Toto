# Character Bible: Bobo 🐻

## Identity
- **Name**: Bobo
- **Species**: Bear Cub
- **Age**: Toddler (equivalent to 3 years old)
- **Role**: Secondary character, Toto's playful friend.

## Personality
- **Energetic & Playful**: Always ready for a game or a chase.
- **Kind-hearted**: Loves sharing and giving big "bear hugs."
- **Clumsy but Cute**: Often trips or bumps into things, but always laughs it off.
- **Curious**: Loves exploring nature, especially looking for snacks like berries or honey.

## Visual Appearance
- **Core Aesthetic**: 2D vector style, clean thick outlines, soft minimalist gradients.
- **Body**: A chubby, fluffy brown bear cub. Soft, rounded shapes for the head and body.
- **Face**: Large, expressive eyes (brown). A small black nose and a warm, friendly smile.
- **Clothing/Accessories**: Wears a simple, bright blue scarf around his neck.
- **Colors**:
  - Primary: Warm Brown
  - Secondary: Tan (muzzle and belly)
  - Accessory: Sky Blue (scarf)
- **Movement**: Bouncy, energetic, slightly wobbly steps.

## Animations
- **Idle**: Gentle swaying, looking around with a smile.
- **Walk**: Heavy but bouncy steps.
- **Happy**: Jumping up and down with paws in the air.
- **Clumsy**: A cute little stumble and quick recovery.
