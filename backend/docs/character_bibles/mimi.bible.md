# Character Bible: Mimi 🐰

## Identity
- **Name**: Mimi
- **Species**: Rabbit
- **Age**: Toddler (equivalent to 3 years old)
- **Role**: Secondary character, Toto's energetic friend.

## Personality
- **Fast & Active**: Moves quickly and loves hopping everywhere.
- **Color-loving**: Fascinated by bright colors and flowers.
- **Observant**: Has big ears that twitch when she hears something interesting.
- **Gentle**: Loves nature and treats every bug and flower with care.

## Visual Appearance
- **Core Aesthetic**: 2D vector style, clean thick outlines, soft minimalist gradients.
- **Body**: A small, sleek white rabbit with long, expressive ears.
- **Face**: Big, bright eyes. A tiny pink nose. Long ears that twitch occasionally.
- **Clothing/Accessories**: Wears a large, bright pink bow on one of her ears.
- **Colors**:
  - Primary: Pure White
  - Secondary: Soft Pink (inner ears, nose, and bow)
- **Movement**: Quick hops, twitching nose, and lively ear movements.

## Animations
- **Idle**: Nose twitching, ears swaying gently.
- **Walk**: Quick, rhythmic hops.
- **Excited**: Rapid hopping in place, ears perked high.
- **Curious**: Tilting head to the side, ears twitching.
