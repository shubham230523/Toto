# Character Bible: Toto 🐢

## 1. Appearance
Toto is a small, bright green turtle designed with a toddler-friendly aesthetic. He has a soft-edged, rounded body without sharp corners. His shell is a key feature, appearing sturdy but not heavy, featuring a simplified geometric pattern. His most striking feature is his large, expressive brown eyes that convey a sense of wonder and kindness.

## 2. Colors
*   **Skin (Primary):** Vibrant Lime Green (#90EE90) - conveys energy and health.
*   **Shell (Base):** Warm Earthy Brown (#8B4513) - represents stability and safety.
*   **Shell (Pattern):** Deep Forest Green (#228B22) - simplified hexagonal or circular markings.
*   **Underbelly:** Pale Creamy Yellow (#FFFACD) - soft contrast to the skin.
*   **Eyes:** Rich Chocolate Brown (#4B2312) with high-contrast white highlights for sparkle.

## 3. Body Proportions
*   **Total Height:** Approximately 2 "heads" high.
*   **Head:** Oversized and rounded, roughly 45% of total body height.
*   **Body:** Stout and plump, slightly smaller than the head.
*   **Limbs:** Short, thick, and rounded flipper-like legs. Clumsy but cute.
*   **Shell:** Occupies roughly 70% of the torso area, rounded like a smooth dome.

## 4. Personality
*   **Thoughtful:** Often stops to think before moving or acting.
*   **Curious:** Intrigued by small things like blades of grass, ants, or raindrops.
*   **Gentle:** Moves carefully to avoid hurting anything or anyone.
*   **Observant:** Noticeably tilts his head when looking at new objects.
*   **Slow & Steady:** Never in a rush; his movement rhythm is melodic and relaxed.

## 5. Visual Style
*   **Medium:** 2D Vector-based animation style.
*   **Line Art:** Thick, clean, colored outlines (slightly darker than fill color, never pure black).
*   **Shading:** Minimalist soft gradients. No complex textures or "realistic" rendering.
*   **Vibe:** Bright, clean, "sunny afternoon" feeling. High legibility against varied backgrounds.

## 6. Expressions
*   **Happy:** Wide, closed-eye smile or small open-mouth grin (no teeth).
*   **Surprised:** Eyes become perfect circles, tiny "o" shaped mouth.
*   **Thinking:** One eyebrow raised (if stylized) or looking up with a tilted head and a finger/flipper to the chin.
*   **Sad:** Slightly downturned mouth, eyes drooping slightly, shoulders hunched.
*   **Sleepy:** Heavy eyelids, slow blinking, tiny yawn.
*   **Focused:** Intensely large eyes, mouth set in a straight line.

## 7. Animation Capabilities
*   **Locomotion:** Slow, rhythmic walking with a slight "waddle."
*   **Shell Mechanics:** Can fully retreat into his shell for safety or sleep. Can peek out one eye at a time.
*   **Gestures:** Waving with a flipper, nodding, slow head tilts (left/right).
*   **Interaction:** Picking up small objects (like an apple) with both flippers.
*   **Expression morphing:** Smooth transitions between emotional states.
